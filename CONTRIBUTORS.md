## How to Contribute

We welcome contributions of all levels! To contribute:

1. Read [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for build, testing, and coding standards.
2. Check [good first issues](https://github.com/SneppX/sneppx-alg/labels/good%20first%20issue) for beginner-friendly tasks.
3. Submit pull requests with focused, single-purpose commits following Conventional Commits (`feat:`, `fix:`, `docs:`, `test:`, `chore:`).
4. Verify your changes locally before submitting (per-file pytest and Ninja builds).

All contributors agree to abide by the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
